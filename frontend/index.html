How it works

  1. A donor registers and lists surplus food with an expiry time.
  2. An approved NGO accepts the listing before it expires — the smart contract blocks expired food automatically.
  3. Both donor and NGO confirm pickup on-chain (dual confirmation), then the NGO marks the food distributed.
  4. Every donation gets a Food Passport — a QR code that anyone can scan to see its full, tamper-resistant history.
